AI Interview Arena

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains how AI Interview Arena collects and uses information when you create an account and use interview practice, resume review, coding practice, voice interview, and Study Plan features.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, password hash, Google account identifier when you use Google Sign-In, and optional phone or verification details.
  • Practice history: interview questions and answers, roles and languages, scores, feedback, and related progress saved to your account.
  • Study Plan and payments: selected language and access dates, plus the name, email, amount, UPI transaction reference (UTR), and review status submitted for manual payment verification.
  • Resume content: when you upload a resume for a review or interview, the app extracts its text in memory and sends relevant text to the configured AI service to produce the requested response. The app does not save the uploaded resume file or extracted resume text.
  • Voice and coding practice: voice answer transcripts are saved with interview history; the app does not store audio. Coding editor contents and some practice progress are stored in your browser. Code you choose to run is sent to the hosted Wandbox compiler with its save option disabled.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate your account, provide and personalize practice features, save your interview history and Study Plan access, review payment references, send account verification emails, maintain platform security, and respond to support requests.

Google Sign-In

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google provides the identity information needed to authenticate you, such as your name, email address, and a stable account identifier. We use it to create or access your AI Interview Arena account. We do not receive your Google password or access your Gmail, contacts, Drive, or other Google content through this sign-in feature.

Service providers and sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share only the information needed with service providers that help operate requested features: Google for sign-in, MongoDB for account and interview records, Azure AI Foundry for AI responses, Wandbox for code execution, Brevo for verification emails, and Vercel and Render for website and server hosting. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the service and its users.

Storage, security, and retention

Account, interview, and payment-request records are stored in the platform database. Passwords are stored as hashes. Login tokens and some editor or progress data are kept in your browser storage; clearing the browser's site data signs you out and may remove locally stored practice progress. We retain account records while the account is active and handle deletion requests through the contact below. Payment-verification records may be retained as needed to resolve payment or access disputes and meet applicable record-keeping requirements.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to use Google Sign-In, resume uploads, voice practice, or paid Study Plan features. To ask a question, request access to or deletion of your account information, or raise a privacy concern, email asijayetin@gmail.com. We may need to verify your account before acting on a request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version and its update date will be available on this page.
